Mr. Amit Khurana

DIRECTOR OF JSB BUILDWELL PVT LTD.

Mr. Amit Khurana is a second generation entrepreneur in the field of construction and is now the Director of JSB Buildwell Pvt Ltd. Born and bought up in Delhi, Amit started his career in 2010 and joined his father in the business. He was instrumental in turning a family business into a professionally driven organization. Thus, currently JSB Buildwell is having very well laid out processes and competencies in real estate Development & Construction. The company has office in Heart of city Gole Market- New Delhi and developing multiple projects in Delhi NCR.

Amit graduated from Delhi University and as he had keen interest, he also studied Astrology and has an Acharya Degree in Astrology from Bharti Vidya Bhawan.

Amit is passionate about his work and believes in the principle of “Pursuit of Perfection”. He picks one project with the belief to deliver to a level where the project surpasses the expectations of everyone involved. Each and every delivered project has become the landmark in his career.

On the personal front, Amit is happily married and has three beautiful children. He and his family love travelling and watching movies together. (In case we want to put a personal information)
